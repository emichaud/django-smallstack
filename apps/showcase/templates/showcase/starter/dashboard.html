Your own card types

The Trend chart card in the dashboards' Add card dialog comes from this project, not SmallStack: apps/website/dashboard_cards.py registers it with register_component_type. Copy that file to add a chart, a table or a gauge. Its data comes from metric_trend, which is also available at GET /smallstack/dashboards/api/metrics/<key>/trend/.

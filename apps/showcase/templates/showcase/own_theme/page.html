SmallStack on your own theme

This page doesn't extend smallstack/base.html. Its base template is a stand-in for a project's own theme, with its own fonts, colours, .card and .btn-primary. SmallStack's embeds draw on it with two tags in that base: {% templatetag openblock %} smallstack_assets {% templatetag closeblock %} in the head and {% templatetag openblock %} smallstack_modal {% templatetag closeblock %} before </body>.
